## Objective and scope

Give customers and personnel individual accounts, authenticated sessions and server-enforced access to their own or explicitly authorized records. Customer self-registration includes email confirmation. Management can administer instructors; staff can create customers and edit their own personal details. Both frontends keep Spanish primary and English available.

Identity owns accounts, roles, permissions, family membership and its membership-change interface. Instructor catalog/media/schedules remain studio-catalog responsibilities; percentages belong to their authorized domain workflow. Credit disposition and reservations remain credits-bookings responsibilities. Account/profile administration must not introduce those financial workflows or erase their future history.

Confirmed staff attendance benefit: all staff and instructors may book any class for free. At capacity, a paying customer can still book by automatically replacing the most recently made eligible complimentary staff/instructor reservation, with a notification to the displaced person; earlier complimentary reservations retain priority. Free staff/instructor attendance consumes no paid credit and generates no instructor pay. This benefit is separate from manually granted customer courtesy credits with an explicitly assigned payroll value. Identity supplies active staff/instructor eligibility; credits-bookings owns complimentary booking, capacity priority and displacement, and instructor-payroll must exclude this attendance benefit from earnings. Notification delivery and timestamp tie-breaking require their owning specifications. The benefit does not require staff to purchase a customer package; the precise account/role representation remains a technical design decision.

## Confirmed account permissions

| Action | Admin/management | Staff without management role | Customer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Create instructor | Allowed | Denied | Denied |
| Update another instructor | Allowed | Denied | Denied |
| Delete another instructor | Allowed | Denied | Denied |
| Create, edit or deactivate another non-instructor staff account | Allowed | Denied | Denied |
| Update own personal profile | Allowed | Allowed | Allowed |
| Create customer via email invitation | Allowed | Allowed, including instructors | Self-registration with email confirmation |
| Create account without requiring email confirmation | Admin and management allowed | Denied | Denied |
| Delete another customer | Allowed | Denied | Denied |
| Delete own customer account | Allowed when acting as that customer | Allowed when acting as that customer | Allowed |
| Update another customer's personal details | Allowed | Denied, including customers they created | Denied |
| Assign or change account roles through authorized role administration | Allowed | Denied | Denied |
| Change own role/permissions/pay rate through profile editing | Denied | Denied | Denied |

Owner/admin/manager retain their previously agreed operational authority. Only admin/management may assign or change account roles through an authorized role-administration operation; profile editing and customer invitations cannot grant roles. Confirmed: reject deactivation or role removal that would leave no active account with administrative/management access, including changes to the acting user's own account. Enforce this invariant transactionally so concurrent changes cannot remove the last two administrative accounts simultaneously; the locking strategy belongs in planning. Other safeguards for privileged changes still need specification. An instructor's self-profile update is distinct from administering another instructor. Instructors are explicitly allowed to create customers through email invitations; this grants no additional permission to edit or deactivate those customers.

Apply authorization in the API for every operation and target identifier, regardless of which frontend sends it. Hiding a control is not authorization. Reject mass-assignment of roles, verification status, pay rates or ownership identifiers. Sensitive account mutations and audit events commit together. Never put password hashes, passwords, sessions or confirmation/reset tokens in audit payloads.

Confirmed: admin/management may view the full operational audit. Customers and instructors may view only their own relevant transactions and payment history; do not expose raw audit payloads or other private records through these views. Ordinary staff have no full-audit permission unless another assigned role authorizes it. Enforce access and record scope in the API, including direct requests and altered identifiers. Confirmed: preserve booking, payment, payroll and audit history after deactivation, with no automatic deletion in the first release. Handle personal-data erasure/anonymization requests separately while preserving required financial records. Define production retention periods before launch; this is not a legal retention determination or permission to retain all personal data indefinitely.

Confirmed editable personal profile fields: name, phone, preferred language and profile photo. Apply the existing self/other-account editing permissions to these fields. Email changes require confirmation of the new address through a separate account-security workflow; profile editing cannot assign roles, privileges or verification status. Specify validation and photo storage/upload limits during planning.

Confirmed: all accounts sign in with email, including admin and testuser. Their names are display/account names, not alternate login identifiers. The first admin is created manually in PostgreSQL with an email address and no email-verification step. Planning must provide a reviewed manual bootstrap procedure using the normal password-hashing format; never store a plaintext password or automatically create the first admin during migrations/startup. Production bootstrap credentials are supplied separately from the public development credentials below.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| testuser | testuser@conecta.example.test | testuser | Customer |

These are intentionally public local test credentials, not production bootstrap credentials. The first admin is manually bootstrapped as requested. An admin can then create the development testuser without email confirmation, with password testuser matching its account name. Confirmed: name-based initial passwords apply only to development test accounts (admin/admin and testuser/testuser), never to regular admin/management-created accounts. Regular admin/management-created accounts receive a secure, single-use email link to choose their own password, including when email confirmation is exempted. Password setup does not remove the confirmation exemption or constitute a separate confirmation requirement.

Any development-fixture helper defined during planning must require both development environment and separate fixture opt-in, reject production/staging/unknown environments, and target a clearly identified local/disposable database. It must not automatically bootstrap the first admin or run from migrations, normal startup or production deployment.

Store fixture passwords through the same password-hashing path as other accounts. Repeated seeding must not overwrite a real account or reset an existing account's credentials/roles. Production must reject development-fixture identities even if a development database was accidentally copied. Any shorter-password exception is limited to these fixtures and cannot be used by public registration.

The fixture email addresses and roles above are approved for development. Customer self-registration still requires confirmation. Admin-created accounts have an explicit confirmation exemption; store that exemption/provenance distinctly from proof that the mailbox was verified. Normal staff/instructor invitation flows do not receive this exemption.

## Proposed authentication flows (review required)

- Customer registers with personal details, email and password. Server assigns only the customer role. Pending accounts have no operational access until email confirmation.
Confirmed email-link policy: confirmation and initial password setup expire after 24 hours; password recovery after 1 hour; family, guardian and group-responsibility invitations after 7 days. All links are single-use. Resending invalidates the previous link, requires a 60-second wait and allows at most 5 sends per hour per recipient and purpose. Use a local email inbox in development; select the production provider during implementation planning. Test expiry boundaries, single use, resend invalidation and both resend limits.

- Confirmation links are time-limited and single-use; resend is throttled. Email comparison/uniqueness rules apply consistently to registration, invitations, login, recovery and email changes. A duplicate email must not attach an account to someone else's customer history.
- Confirmed: customer creation requires email. The normal personnel-created flow invites the customer to confirm their email and set their password before account access is enabled. An admin or manager may instead explicitly create an account without requiring email confirmation; the development testuser uses its name as its password. Regular admin/management-created accounts receive a secure, single-use email link to choose their own password, even when confirmation is exempted; account access requires completing password setup. Ordinary staff and instructors cannot assign passwords or bypass confirmation. The confirmed email-link policy above applies; production provider selection belongs in implementation planning.
- Include login, logout, current-account retrieval and password recovery. Proposed web transport is an opaque server-managed session in an HttpOnly cookie, Secure on HTTPS, with explicit SameSite/CSRF and origin rules for both frontends. Confirmed: one login works across both frontends, with assigned roles controlling access. Sessions expire 7 days after sign-in, or 30 days with Remember me selected. Password reset, account deactivation and role changes invalidate existing sessions; users can sign out of all devices. Plan shared authentication transport explicitly across frontend origins; test both session durations, role boundaries and all-device revocation.
- Confirmed: all active accounts may recover their password through a secure, single-use email link, including admin/management-created confirmation-exempt accounts. Recovery cannot reactivate a deactivated account or change roles. Apply the confirmed email-link expiration and resend limits. Test that a used link cannot be reused and that recovery does not enable access for inactive accounts.
- Passwords use an adaptive salted hash, with Argon2id as the proposed choice; select a maintained Node-compatible implementation and parameters during planning. Do not use the development passwords to set the normal password policy.
- Login/confirmation/recovery need rate limits and responses that do not expose account existence. Email changes require confirmation of the new address; password reset, disabling and privilege changes must invalidate affected sessions.
- Spanish/English labels and emails, understandable validation errors, keyboard access, focus management and existing branded shared components apply to every account screen.

Security references: [OWASP password storage](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Password_Storage_Cheat_Sheet.html), [authentication](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Authentication_Cheat_Sheet.html), [sessions](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Session_Management_Cheat_Sheet.html), [email verification](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Email_Validation_and_Verification_Cheat_Sheet.html). These guide implementation safeguards; they do not establish product permissions.

## Deletion and family boundaries

Confirmed staff/instructor lifecycle: account deactivation immediately revokes login access and complimentary staff booking eligibility. Management must reassign or explicitly cancel upcoming teaching sessions; Identity must not silently cancel studio sessions. Earned instructor pay remains owed and recorded, and management may record its payment after deactivation. Removing staff/instructor roles while retaining an active customer account preserves customer access; distinguish role removal from whole-account deactivation. Studio-catalog owns teaching-session resolution, credits-bookings owns existing complimentary attendance reservations, and instructor-payroll owns accrued earnings and payment records. Test access revocation and retained customer access; verify teaching-session and payroll consequences with their owning modules. Confirmed: when role removal ends complimentary staff/instructor eligibility, cancel future complimentary attendance bookings without charging credits; preserve paid customer bookings when the customer account stays active. Evaluate all remaining roles before ending eligibility. Verify cancellation and eligibility transactionally with credits-bookings.

Confirmed: only admin/management may reactivate a deactivated account. Preserve historical references and audit the actor, time and change. Reactivation does not restore expired credits or cancelled bookings; any permitted credit restoration uses the separately audited manual-adjustment workflow. Login, registration and password recovery must not bypass this permission. Test direct API denials for ordinary staff, instructors and customers, and successful authorized reactivation without resurrecting expired credits or cancelled bookings when credits-bookings exists. Confirmed: reactivation does not automatically restore family membership or guardian authorization. Require a new invitation issued through the existing authorized flows and accepted by the reactivated account before restoring either relationship. Preserve historical relationship records. Test that reactivation alone grants no family credit-sharing or dependent access, pending invitations grant no access, and acceptance restores only the invited relationship. Confirmed: only admin/management may reactivate a dependent profile, and at least one guardian authorized immediately before dependent deactivation must have an active account and accept renewed authorization first. If all qualifying prior guardians are inactive, reject dependent reactivation; a new unrelated guardian cannot satisfy this prerequisite. Preserve the pre-deactivation guardian relationships as provenance rather than using any person who was ever a guardian. Renewed authorization may be accepted while dependent reactivation is pending, but grants no dependent access until admin/management completes reactivation. Coordinate reactivation and guardian activation transactionally so no active dependent is left without an active authorized guardian. Test admin/management authorization, accepted active prior guardian success, all prior guardians inactive denial, pending/declined invitation denial, and unrelated or previously removed guardian denial. Do not restore expired credits or cancelled bookings.

Customer self-deletion and management deletion rights are confirmed. The user confirmed deletion means revoke access and deactivate the profile while preserving booking, payment and audit history. No hard-delete or anonymization timeline is approved. Proposed safeguard: require recent authentication for self-deletion and explicit confirmation for account-deactivation actions.

Confirmed: customer account deactivation cancels that customer's future bookings under the normal two-hour cancellation rule, evaluated at deactivation time: at least two hours before class releases reserved credits; less than two hours consumes them. Preserve booking, payment and audit history. Identity supplies the deactivation interface; credits-bookings coordinates cancellation and credit consequences. Confirmed: unused credits remain recorded until normal expiration, without an automatic refund or validity extension. Family-held credits follow the agreed family-departure rules, including the purchaser choice to withdraw eligible credits or leave them with the group. During customer self-deactivation, require an explicit choice to keep eligible purchased credits recorded on the inactive account or leave them with the family, without a refund. Credits retain their normal expiration; credits reserved for still-valid bookings remain attached to those bookings. Confirmed: management-initiated deactivation without a customer choice keeps eligible purchased credits recorded on the inactive account, with no refund and unchanged expiration; it does not donate those credits to the family. Reserved credits attached to still-valid bookings remain reserved. Confirmed: closing a family returns remaining unused/unreserved credits to their original purchasers, including credits previously left with the group, without changing monetary value or expiration. Credits reserved for valid bookings remain attached; closure does not restore consumed or expired credits or issue refunds. Earned instructor pay remains owed and recorded; management may record payment after deactivation, through instructor-payroll. Do not introduce additional credit forfeiture, balance transfers, pay settlement or loss of historical references. Confirmed: when a guardian deactivates but another active authorized guardian remains, the dependent stays active and their bookings remain valid, including bookings created by the departing guardian. When the dependent is also deactivated, cancel their future bookings under the same two-hour rule, preserving history. Retention follows the confirmed history-preservation policy above; production periods and personal-data erasure/anonymization procedures must be defined before launch. Deactivation is not a claim that all personal data has been erased.

Keep the approved family rules: individual logins for independent members and parent-managed dependent profiles without their own email/login, automatic sharing while in a family, withdrawal limited to the departing purchaser's unused/unreserved credits, optional donation, and reservation preservation. Identity supplies membership facts and a transactional membership-change interface; credits-bookings coordinates financial disposition without a reverse dependency from Identity. Confirmed: customers may create their own family group and invite members; every invitation requires acceptance before membership and credit sharing begin. Admin/management may assist with family-group management, but invitations still require acceptance. Confirmed: a parent manages a dependent profile and books classes for that dependent using the parent account. The booking identifies the dependent as attendee and the parent as the acting user. Dependents do not require separate email accounts; invitations for independent accounts still require acceptance. Detailed guardian permissions, technical details of the confirmed independent-account transition, remaining management operations and how deletion triggers departure require specification; successor acceptance and last-member closure follow the confirmed decisions below. Do not ship an uncoordinated leave/delete route that will break these rules.

## Acceptance scenarios

1. The reviewed manual first-admin bootstrap creates an email-login administrator with an explicit confirmation exemption. Development admin/testuser use email logins and the requested development passwords. An admin can create testuser without confirmation; non-development use of fixture identities/credentials is rejected. Fixture helpers cannot overwrite existing accounts or automatically bootstrap the first admin.
2. Self-registration creates only an unconfirmed customer; confirmation succeeds once, rejects expired/reused links and grants no staff role.
3. Admin/management can administer another instructor. Ordinary staff/customer direct API attempts are denied, including altered target IDs and forged role fields.
4. Staff can create a customer and edit their own allowed personal fields, but cannot delete a customer or edit another customer/staff/instructor profile. This denial also applies to customer records they created. Only admin/management may create, edit or deactivate other non-instructor staff accounts; test denials for ordinary staff and customers through direct API calls. Admin/management may edit another customer's allowed personal fields; profile editing never grants role or credential control.
5. Customers can edit/delete only themselves. A forged account ID or privileged profile field does not broaden access.
   Role administration succeeds only for admin/management; direct API role-change attempts from ordinary staff, instructors and customers are denied. Customer registration/invitations assign only the customer role, regardless of submitted role fields.
   Deactivation or role removal of the last active administrative account is rejected without changing access. With two active administrative accounts, concurrent changes must still leave at least one active administrative account; test self-directed changes as well as changes to another account.
6. Personnel-created customers require email. Test successful normal invitations from admin/management, reception, café staff and instructors. Invitations let customers confirm their address and choose their password. Missing email is rejected. Separately test admin/management-created confirmation exemptions, with audited actor/provenance and no false mailbox-verification claim. Regular admin/management-created accounts receive single-use password setup links and cannot sign in before setting a password; using a consumed link is rejected, including for confirmation-exempt accounts. Ordinary staff, instructors and self-registering customers cannot invoke that exemption or expose private records. Instructor invitation rights do not permit subsequent profile edits or deactivation.
7. Logout, password reset, account disablement and role changes invalidate sessions as specified; session/CSRF boundaries are tested across both origins.
8. Account mutations and sanitized audit history are atomic. Customer deactivation cancels future bookings using the normal two-hour rule, with cases before, exactly at and within the cutoff. Preserve historical references and record cancellation/credit outcomes without additional forfeiture, automatic refunds or unapproved transfers. Verify unused credits retain their normal expiration and family credits follow the agreed departure rules. Verify transactional coordination and rollback with credits-bookings when that module exists; do not ship a route that bypasses these obligations.
9. Both languages and all three Foundation viewports pass account-flow keyboard, validation, loading, error and retry checks.
10. Multi-role and family behavior follows the final decisions below. Customers can create a family and invite members; admin/management can assist with management. Invited people are not added to the group or granted shared-credit access until they accept, including invitations issued with management assistance. Test that authorized parents can manage their dependent profiles, that dependents need no email/login, and that unrelated family members cannot manage them solely through shared family membership. Test guardian invitations from an existing authorized guardian and with admin/management assistance: pending invitations grant no profile access or booking authority; acceptance grants only the intended dependent authorization. Test that only admin/management can remove another guardian, guardians can remove themselves, and neither sequential nor concurrent removals can leave an active dependent with zero active authorized guardians; pending invitations do not count. Test accepted guardianship transfer before account deactivation and explicit joint guardian/dependent deactivation with history preserved. Test that deleting one guardian account does not deactivate the dependent or revoke access for other active guardians. Joint guardian/dependent deactivation is allowed only when no other active authorized guardian remains. Verify that dependent bookings, including those created by the departing guardian, remain valid when another active guardian remains. If the dependent is also deactivated, verify cancellation under the two-hour rule with history preserved. Bookings must identify the dependent attendee separately from the parent actor. Test closure permissions for the current responsible member and admin/management; verify that a departed creator loses closure permission and transferring group responsibility does not change credit ownership or dependent guardianship. Test that pending or declined successor offers grant no closure authority, acceptance grants authority to the intended remaining member, decline does not block creator departure, and the last remaining member can close the group when no successor has accepted. Deny closure to other unauthorized members. Audit closure and verify credit disposition with credits-bookings. Family-credit integration must be verified again when credits-bookings is implemented.

No numeric coverage threshold is invented. Use Node unit tests, isolated PostgreSQL integration tests and real Chromium flows; assert permission denials at the API, not just absent buttons.
